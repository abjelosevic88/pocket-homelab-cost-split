# Roadmap

Things that are deliberately not in v0.1, roughly in the order they might come.

- **Receipts.** Attach a photo or PDF to an expense (stored under `DATA_DIR/receipts`).
- **Recurring expenses.** Rent and subscriptions that repeat monthly/weekly, like Spliit's.
- **Share links.** A read-only (or add-only) link for people without an account, so a trip group works without creating users.
- **Notifications.** "You were added to an expense" / "Jelena marked your payment as received" through Apprise, ntfy or a webhook, like the sibling apps.
- **Per-group default split.** Remember the last split mode and participants per group.
- **Multi-currency balances on Home.** Convert every group's net into one currency at today's rate, as an estimate next to the exact per-currency figures.
- **Settle in another currency.** A reimbursement paid in cash abroad already works (it carries its own rate); a "pay in" shortcut on the suggestion could pick the currency.
- **Import from Splitwise / Spliit exports.**

Not planned: shared links with public write access, bank synchronisation, a mobile app beyond the PWA.
